📊 How Forecasting Works

The model fits a straight line of best fit through your daily cumulative spending data points. The equation represents Y = m * X + c, where Y is total money spent, X is the number of days elapsed, m is the slope (daily rate), and c is the intercept.

R² (R-Squared) measures how close your actual daily spending aligns to this line. An R² close to 1 indicates uniform, steady daily expenditures. Lower values represent volatile, random spending spikes.
